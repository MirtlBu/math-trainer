Equations

Choose a type to practice

Beginner

Linear Equations — Basic

One-step equations like $x + 5 = 12$, $2x = 8$ or $\frac{x}{3} = 4$

Intermediate

Linear Equations — Two-step and Both Sides

Equations like $3x + 4 = 19$, $\frac{x}{2} - 3 = 5$ or $5x - 3 = 2x + 9$

Intermediate

Equations with Fractions — Simple

Multiply every term by the common denominator: $\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{x}{3} = 5$ or $\dfrac{x}{3} + 2 = 7$

Intermediate

Systems of Equations — Substitution and Elimination

Systems like $y = 2x - 1,\ 3x + y = 14$ or $3x + 2y = 7,\ 3x - y = 1$: put one equation into the other, or add or subtract them

Intermediate

Quadratic Equations — Factored and Incomplete

Equations like $(x - 2)(x + 5) = 0$, $x^2 = 49$ or $3x^2 - 12x = 0$

Advanced

Equations with Fractions — Brackets and x in the Denominator

Equations like $\dfrac{x + 1}{3} = \dfrac{x - 2}{4}$ or $\dfrac{3}{x} = \dfrac{5}{x + 4}$ (remember $x \neq 0$)

Advanced

Systems of Equations — Multiplying First

Systems like $2x + 3y = 12,\ 3x - 4y = 1$: multiply the equations to match the coefficients

Advanced

Quadratic, Cubic and Quartic Equations

Equations like $x^2 + 5x + 6 = 0$, $x^3 - 3x^2 - 4x + 12 = 0$ or $x^4 - 5x^2 + 4 = 0$

Expressions

Choose a type to practice

Beginner

Expanding Brackets — Number × Brackets

Expressions like $4 \cdot (3x - 2)$ or $(5 + 2x) \cdot (-3)$

Beginner

Roots — Simplifying a Root

Expressions like $\sqrt{54}$ or $2\sqrt{48}$

Intermediate

Expanding Brackets — Monomials and Binomials

Expressions like $4x \cdot (3x - 2)$ or $(4x - 2) \cdot (3x + 2)$

Intermediate

Roots — Adding, Subtracting, Multiplying and Dividing

Expressions like $\sqrt{54} - \sqrt{24}$, $2\sqrt{3} \cdot 3\sqrt{6}$ or $\dfrac{\sqrt{98}}{\sqrt{2}}$

Advanced

Roots — Brackets and Rationalizing

Expressions like $(2 + \sqrt{3})^2$ or $\dfrac{10}{3 + \sqrt{5}}$

Advanced

Inequalities — Factored, Quadratic, Cubic and Quartic

Functions & Graphs

Linear Graphs and Functions — Reading

Intermediate

Linear Functions — Comparing and Interpreting

Two equations like $D = 6t + 10$ and $E = 3t + 20$: when are they equal? Or choose the statement that matches the graph

Word Problems

Choose a type to practice

Beginner

Percent of a Quantity

How much is 25% of €300? How many grams is 0.1% of 20 kg? What percent is 45 of 300?

Intermediate

Word Problems — Setting Up Equations

Parts of a whole, consecutive numbers, perimeter, two kinds of items: write the equation from the story

Intermediate

Percent Problems — Intermediate

Percent change, comparing two values (which one is the base?), true statements, percentage points, rounding, "if $x$ grows by 1.2%, the new value is…"

Intermediate

Linear Models from Data

No equation given: build the model from the data (e.g. a battery charged from 23% to 89% in 30 min) and find the speed and the time

Intermediate

Direct and Inverse Proportion

Braking distance and speed, workers and days: suoraan and kääntäen verrannollisuus

Advanced

Word Problems — Area and Products

Quadratic equations from a story, where one root has to be rejected: $x(x + 3) = 40$

Advanced

Percent Problems — Advanced

Finding the original value, two changes in a row (+10%, then −10%), compound change, mixtures

Advanced

Containers and Consistency

Identical objects in a container (a system of equations), or check whether the numbers in a story contradict each other

Advanced

Which Dependence? — Direct, Inverse or Neither

Read a table of values: is $y$ directly or inversely proportional to $x$? Find the constant $k$
